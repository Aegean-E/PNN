# Data and Asset Manifest

The tables in `data/` are project-authored: blank assay, docking, dynamics, preclinical and
toxicology templates, and illustrative compound libraries with invented identifiers. They are
offered under ASL-1.1 as Project Data. They contain no personal data, no measured results from a
third party and no third-party dataset. No model weights ship.

| Path or pattern | Type | Rights holder | License | Personal data | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `data/**/*_template.csv` | data templates | AegeanWorks | ASL-1.1 | no | blank schemas |
| `data/compounds/*_library.csv`, `data/docking/multi_target_scores.csv` | illustrative tables | AegeanWorks | ASL-1.1 | no | invented compounds and scores |
| `computational/`, `experimental/`, `translational/` | research protocols | AegeanWorks | ASL-1.1 | no | documentation |
